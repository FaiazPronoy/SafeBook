Everything you would expect

What you can do

The parts of a social app people actually use, with a lock built into each one.

{% icon "users" %}

Post to the right people

Pick Friends, Public or Only me for every post, and change it later. The choice decides who gets a key, not just who sees a button.

{% icon "comment" %}

Comment and like

Comments are locked with the same key as the post, so exactly the people who can read a post can read the conversation under it.

{% icon "chat" %}

Chat in private

Each message is sealed for the two people in the chat. Scroll back through your history; SafeBook keeps no key to any of it.

{% icon "shield" %}

Check a friend's key

Every profile shows a safety number. Compare it with a friend in person and you know nobody swapped their key for another.

No fine print

Who can read what

Not a privacy setting on top of a readable database. Each tick is a person holding a key; each cross is somebody who does not have one.

What the server does see: who is friends with whom, who liked what, when things were posted, and your name and bio. And while you are signed in, the server uses your key to show you your own things; sign out and it holds nothing that opens them. Hiding all of that would need the encryption to run in your browser, which is a different kind of app, and SafeBook says so rather than pretend.
