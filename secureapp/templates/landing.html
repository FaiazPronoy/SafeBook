Your password is the only key

The key that opens your notes is locked with your password. Forget it and your recovery code is the one other way in. Nobody at SafeBook can reset it for you.

A message opens for one person

Each message is sealed for the person you send it to. Compare safety numbers once and you know nobody swapped a key in between.

Tampering is refused, not shown

Every row carries a seal of its own. If a single byte changes in the database, the note is kept off the screen instead of shown with a warning beside it.
