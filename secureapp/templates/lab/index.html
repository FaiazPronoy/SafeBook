How it works

Claims are easy. Here is the working.

Any app can put a padlock in the corner and tell you it is safe. These pages let you watch the difference for yourself, on data made up on the spot. Nothing here touches a real account.

The short answer

Your password builds the key to your notes. While you are signed in, SafeBook can open them for you. When you are not, it holds nothing it can read, and neither does anyone with access to the server.

Sharing a note is the one thing that changes that, and only for that note.

What we cannot do

Nobody here can read your private notes, reset your password for you, or recover your account without your recovery code. Those are the same limitation seen from three sides, and they are the price of the first paragraph.

While you are signed in, this server does see your notes in order to show them to you. That is worth knowing.
