How it works

Claims are easy. Here is the working.

Any app can put a padlock in the corner and tell you it is safe. These pages let you watch the difference for yourself, on data made up on the spot. Nothing here touches a real account.

The short answer

Every post has a key of its own. You hold a copy, locked with your password. Each friend holds a copy, locked with their own key. Nobody else does, SafeBook included, unless you make the post public.

Chats work the same way, with one copy for each of the two people talking.

What we cannot do

Nobody here can read your friends or only-me posts, reset your password for you, or get you back in without your recovery code. Those are the same limitation seen from three sides.

What the server does know: who your friends are, who liked what, and when you posted. Hiding that would need a different kind of system.
