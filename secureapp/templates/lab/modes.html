Why locking is not one thing

One picture, locked three different ways with the same key. The first way locks each small square on its own, so two identical squares come out identical and the shape of the padlock is still perfectly readable. Reload the page for fresh keys.

{% for panel in panels %} {% endfor %}
The picture has large flat areas, so the original repeats a lot of squares. The first method keeps that count almost exactly, and the repeats are the leak. The other two mix each square into the next, so two identical inputs land on completely different outputs and the count falls to nothing. SafeBook uses the last one.
