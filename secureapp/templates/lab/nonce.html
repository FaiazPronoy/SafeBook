How it works / 02

One repeated nonce, both messages lost

Locking works by mixing your text with a long stream of noise. Every message is supposed to get its own stream. Use the same one twice and the two locked messages cancel each other out, leaving the two originals mixed together. Anyone who knows one of them gets the other for free, without ever touching the key.
